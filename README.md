# Personal Website

## About

This personal website started as a way to showcase my projects and technical experience beyond my resume. Over time, it has evolved into a space where I can explore my interests, share more about myself, and continue learning.

With AI making it easier to generate code, I wanted to use this project as an opportunity to relearn web development fundamentals by building things myself, understanding how they work, and making intentional technical decisions rather than relying on AI to generate the implementation.

Beyond software development, I wanted a place to share the things that make me who I am, including my hobbies, interests, and projects outside of work.

I also hope to integrate Notion into the website, connecting the tools I already use to organize my life and projects with the things I share publicly.

## Features

- **Home page** — Landing page introducing the website.
- **Project portfolio** — Browse projects and view individual project details, including descriptions, motivations, implementation details, technologies used, and future improvements.
- **About Me** — Learn more about me, my background, interests, and contact information.
- **Photography** — Explore photography featured on the website.
- **Navigation** — Navigate between pages through a shared navigation bar.
